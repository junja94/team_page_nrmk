We demonstrated **zero-shot pick-and-place** on our humanoid platform **EIR** at CES 2026 in Las Vegas.
Our humanoid robot EIR can handle an object it has never seen before simply from a natural-language command (e.g. "banana"), powered by a combination of **Vision Foundation Model (VFM)s** that localize the target and compute the optimal grasp pose in real time.
