**Neuromeka AI Lab** is a robotics team bringing learning-based intelligence into real production environments.

We believe the next generation of automation will move beyond rigid, pre-programmed workflows toward robots that perceive, learn, and safely work alongside humans in complex industrial settings.

We build **field-ready AI for industrial robots**: learning-based manipulation, multi-modal perception, and safety-aware control integrated with classical robotics, applied to tasks such as deformable object manipulation and precision assembly. Grounded in safety, reliability, and scalability, our work bridges cutting-edge research and production-grade deployment.
